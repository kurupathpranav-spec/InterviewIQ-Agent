# InterviewIQ – Complete Working Flow

InterviewIQ is an AI-powered interview preparation and performance analysis platform that uses IBM Granite to personalize interview preparation, evaluate answers, and generate an improvement plan.

## 1. Resume Intelligence

The candidate provides their name, target role, experience level, interview focus, and resume information.

The system analyzes the candidate profile and identifies:

- Detected skills
- Strengths
- Areas to improve
- Recommended interview topics
- Role relevance
- Readiness score
- Technical score
- Communication indicators

![Resume Intelligence](screenshots/01-resume-analysis.png)

---

## 2. AI Resume Analysis Results

IBM Granite analyzes the candidate profile according to the selected target role and provides personalized insights.

The results help identify the candidate's current strengths and preparation gaps before starting the mock interview.

![Resume Analysis Results](screenshots/02-resume-results.png)

---

## 3. Interview Setup

The candidate selects:

- Target role
- Experience level
- Interview focus

InterviewIQ then prepares a personalized mock interview based on the selected profile.

![Interview Setup](screenshots/03-interview-setup.png)

---

## 4. Personalized Interview Questions

The system generates a 10-question mock interview containing technical, behavioral, and situational questions.

Questions are tailored to the selected role and candidate skills using role-specific knowledge and IBM Granite.

![Interview Question](screenshots/04-interview-question.png)

---

## 5. Real-Time AI Answer Evaluation

After submitting an answer, IBM Granite evaluates the response and provides:

- Score
- Grade
- Strengths
- Areas for improvement
- Missing keywords
- Model answer guidance
- Follow-up question

This creates a continuous feedback loop throughout the interview.

![AI Feedback](screenshots/05-ai-feedback.png)

---

## 6. Performance Report

After completing the interview, InterviewIQ generates an overall performance report.

The report includes:

- Overall score
- Technical score
- Behavioral score
- Communication score
- Executive summary
- Hiring recommendation
- Top strengths
- Critical gaps

![Performance Report](screenshots/06-performance-report.png)

---

## 7. Personalized Improvement Plan

Based on the interview performance, the system generates a structured improvement plan.

The plan identifies specific areas to work on across multiple stages of preparation.

![Improvement Plan](screenshots/07-improvement-plan.png)

---

## 8. Recommended Learning Resources

InterviewIQ also recommends learning resources based on the candidate's identified skill gaps.

This helps the candidate move from interview assessment to targeted preparation.

![Recommended Resources](screenshots/08-recommended-resources.png)

---
